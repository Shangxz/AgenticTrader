### Method and limitations

Today's ranking was formed independently, before reviewing any earlier weekly entries. We surveyed a third-party model that scores approximately 4,300 U.S.-listed stocks but publishes only its top 25 value names from approximately 1,500 liquid eligible stocks, and cross-checked an S&P 500 quality/value screen. We then checked company-reported results and weighed valuation, business resilience, cash generation, balance-sheet considerations and specific downside risks. This is a broad market survey, **not an exhaustive security-by-security screen of every U.S. public company**; microcaps, illiquid stocks, excluded names and data errors could hide better opportunities. Ranking is qualitative, not a precise intrinsic-value calculation.

The [Sirius screen](https://www.siriusinvestors.com/stock/screener/value-stocks) reports trailing P/E and free-cash-flow (FCF) yield as of November 17, before the November 20 selloff. FCF yield is the screen provider's measure relative to equity value; definitions and financial-period cutoffs may differ by company. The [Ticker Nerd screen](https://tickernerd.com/stock/best-value-stocks/) is dated November 21. None of these is a current executable quote. Verify current prices, denominators, dilution and filings before acting.

### Today's top ideas

**1. Leidos Holdings (NYSE: LDOS) | Confidence: moderate**

Management guided for approximately $1.90 billion of 2026 operating cash flow and $18.0 billion-$18.2 billion of revenue, with a mid-13% adjusted EBITDA margin. [Third-quarter results and guidance](https://investors.leidos.com/news-releases/news-release-details/leidos-delivers-strong-third-quarter-and-enhances-full-year).

The FCF yield may reflect timing and acquisition effects; assess debt and normalized cash conversion before extrapolating.

**2. Zoetis (NYSE: ZTS) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** On November 17 the screen showed 13.3x trailing earnings and a 6.8% FCF yield, alongside its 87/100 quality score. This looks reasonable for a diversified animal-health franchise if current pressure proves temporary, an inference rather than a demonstrated margin of safety.
- **Quality:** Its medicines, vaccines and diagnostics span companion animals and livestock internationally. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was $2.4 billion and reported net income was $591 million. Its breadth helps offset individual product weaknesses. [Company results](https://investor.zoetis.com/news/news-details/2026/Zoetis-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-Highlights-Progress-Advancing-Innovation-Across-Diversified-Portfolio-and-Narrows-Full-Year-2026-Revenue-Guidance-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Adjusted-EPS-Guidance/).

Management expects full-year organic operational revenue to decline 1%-2%. Persistent erosion, product safety issues or a weak pipeline could justify the lower multiple. Adjusted guidance is non-GAAP and should not be confused with reported earnings.

**3. PayPal Holdings (NASDAQ: PYPL) | Confidence: moderate-low**

- **Value:** The November 17 screen showed 10.6x trailing earnings and a 12.6% FCF yield. The price may understate normalized earnings if its branded checkout franchise stabilizes; that is a thesis, not a forecast.
- **Quality:** The two-sided payments platform had 440 million active accounts and $487.1 billion in quarterly payment volume. Third-quarter operating cash flow was $1.6 billion and FCF was $1.4 billion; cash, equivalents and investments were $15.1 billion against $13.5 billion of debt at September 30. [PayPal's quarterly filing exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000086/pypl3q-26earningsrelease.htm).
- **Disconfirmation:** Competitive pressure on branded checkout remains substantial. Revenue grew only 2% year over year, GAAP operating income fell 8%, and quarterly FCF fell 17%. BNPL receivable timing affects cash flow; adjusted FCF excludes a timing item and is not interchangeable with reported FCF. [Competition context](https://apnews.com/article/50054e5db0c773c8fe9a437708e1d3a9).
