We surveyed a [U.S.-listed value and quality screen](https://stockunlock.com/stock-lists/potentially-undervalued-stocks.html) displaying 25 names, a [large-company quality and DCF screen](https://stockanaliz.com/lists/undervalued-stocks/), and a [broad U.S. model](https://www.atlas-stocks.com/stocks/best/undervalued/us) describing a universe of 4,654 listings. We weighed valuation, durability, cash generation, balance-sheet considerations and downside, and checked company disclosures. Cyclical semiconductor and commodity names can appear inexpensive on unusually strong earnings; low multiples alone were not decisive.

**This was a limited broad-market survey, not an exhaustive security-by-security scan of the full U.S. public market.** Different universes, incomplete screen results, smaller or illiquid securities, exclusions and data errors could conceal stronger candidates. Ranking is qualitative, not a calculated intrinsic value. Equity free-cash-flow (FCF) yield means trailing operating cash flow less capital spending, divided by equity market value; it is not a promised return and does not deduct stock compensation or normalize working capital.

### Today's top ideas

**1. Adobe (NASDAQ: ADBE) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/adbe/statistics/) reports November 27 market capitalization of $91.38 billion and trailing FCF of $10.59 billion, implying about 8.6x FCF or an 11.6% equity FCF yield. These provider figures are not normalized owner earnings. If its subscriptions remain durable, the price may understate future cash generation.
- **Quality:** Fiscal Q3 revenue rose 13% year over year to $6.76 billion and operating cash flow was $2.52 billion. Creative and document workflows, customer relationships and subscriptions support the business, although durability is an inference. [SEC earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000147/adbeex991q326.htm).
- **Risks and tests:** Generative AI and cheaper competitors could weaken retention and pricing; AI costs and stock compensation can reduce owner economics. Monitor recurring revenue, margins, dilution and sustained cash generation. A trailing yield does not establish a margin of safety.

**2. Intuit (NASDAQ: INTU) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** The [Stock Unlock screen](https://stockunlock.com/stock-lists/potentially-undervalued-stocks.html), dated November 25, showed 15.1x trailing earnings and 8.1x trailing FCF. These are older third-party measures, not November 27 ratios; [November 27 market capitalization](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/) was about $71.65 billion. The price may be attractive if tax and small-business software economics persist.
- **Quality:** Fiscal Q1 2027 revenue grew 12% year over year and Global Business Solutions revenue grew 15%. QuickBooks and TurboTax are embedded in customer workflows, supporting switching costs, though that advantage is not assured. [Intuit 10-Q](https://investors.intuit.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000896878-26-000045/intu-20261031.htm).
- **Risks and tests:** AI-assisted alternatives could pressure pricing; Credit Karma depends on consumer-credit conditions. Intuit reduced its revenue outlook and agreed to sell Mailchimp, so historical cash flow and guidance may not represent the post-sale business. Review sale terms, updated guidance, debt, tax-season share and dilution. [Company results](https://investors.intuit.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000896878-26-000043/fy27q1earningspressrelease.htm).

**3. PayPal Holdings (NASDAQ: PYPL) | Confidence: moderate-low**

- **Value:** [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/PYPL/key-statistics/) reports approximately $48.02 billion market capitalization and 10.54x trailing earnings at the November 27 close. [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/statistics/) reports $6.30 billion trailing FCF, implying roughly 13.1% yield against Yahoo's equity value. These are different providers and share-count or cutoff differences may affect the calculation.
- **Quality:** Its two-sided merchant and consumer network provides scale; Q3 payment volume was $487.1 billion, operating cash flow was $1.6 billion and FCF was $1.4 billion. Cash, equivalents and investments were $15.1 billion versus $13.5 billion of debt; that comparison does not establish that all cash is unrestricted or excess. [PayPal Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000086/pypl3q-26earningsrelease.htm).
- **Risks and tests:** Branded checkout competition, mix shifts and credit exposure could impair economics. Q3 FCF fell 17% year over year and buy-now-pay-later receivable timing affects cash conversion. Track checkout growth, transaction margins, funding needs and cash flow through a full cycle. [Competition context](https://apnews.com/article/50054e5db0c773c8fe9a437708e1d3a9).

PayPal offers a higher apparent yield with greater competitive uncertainty. These are research candidates, not verified discounts, guaranteed outcomes or personalized financial advice.
