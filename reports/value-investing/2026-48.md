### Method and limitations

Today's ranking was formed independently, before reviewing any earlier weekly entries. We surveyed a third-party model that scores approximately 4,300 U.S.-listed stocks but publishes only its top 25 value names from approximately 1,500 liquid eligible stocks, and cross-checked an S&P 500 quality/value screen. We then checked company-reported results and weighed valuation, business resilience, cash generation, balance-sheet considerations and specific downside risks. This is a broad market survey, **not an exhaustive security-by-security screen of every U.S. public company**; microcaps, illiquid stocks, excluded names and data errors could hide better opportunities. Ranking is qualitative, not a precise intrinsic-value calculation.

The [Sirius screen](https://www.siriusinvestors.com/stock/screener/value-stocks) reports trailing P/E and free-cash-flow (FCF) yield as of November 17, before the November 20 selloff. FCF yield is the screen provider's measure relative to equity value; definitions and financial-period cutoffs may differ by company. The [Ticker Nerd screen](https://tickernerd.com/stock/best-value-stocks/) is dated November 21. None of these is a current executable quote. Verify current prices, denominators, dilution and filings before acting.

### Today's top ideas

**1. Leidos Holdings (NYSE: LDOS) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** The November 17 screen showed 11.4x trailing earnings and a 12.1% FCF yield. Its November 21 value score was 99.6/100 in the separate broad-universe model. The discount may be excessive if government mission demand and cash conversion persist, but neither screen establishes fair value.
- **Quality:** Leidos supplies technology and services to government and commercial customers. Management guided for approximately $1.90 billion of 2026 operating cash flow and $18.0 billion-$18.2 billion of revenue, with a mid-13% adjusted EBITDA margin. [Third-quarter results and guidance](https://investors.leidos.com/news-releases/news-release-details/leidos-delivers-strong-third-quarter-and-enhances-full-year).
- **Disconfirmation:** Government budget changes, shutdowns, procurement delays and contract losses could impair revenue and margins. Revenue guidance was reduced from $18.2 billion-$18.4 billion even as cash-flow guidance rose. The FCF yield may reflect timing and acquisition effects; assess debt and normalized cash conversion before extrapolating.

**2. Zoetis (NYSE: ZTS) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** On November 17 the screen showed 13.3x trailing earnings and a 6.8% FCF yield, alongside its 87/100 quality score. This looks reasonable for a diversified animal-health franchise if current pressure proves temporary, an inference rather than a demonstrated margin of safety.
- **Quality:** Its medicines, vaccines and diagnostics span companion animals and livestock internationally. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was $2.4 billion and reported net income was $591 million. Its breadth helps offset individual product weaknesses. [Company results](https://investor.zoetis.com/news/news-details/2026/Zoetis-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-Highlights-Progress-Advancing-Innovation-Across-Diversified-Portfolio-and-Narrows-Full-Year-2026-Revenue-Guidance-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Adjusted-EPS-Guidance/).
- **Disconfirmation:** U.S. companion-animal revenue fell 8% in the quarter, reflecting price sensitivity, dermatology competition and generics. Management expects full-year organic operational revenue to decline 1%-2%. Persistent erosion, product safety issues or a weak pipeline could justify the lower multiple. Adjusted guidance is non-GAAP and should not be confused with reported earnings.

**3. PayPal Holdings (NASDAQ: PYPL) | Confidence: moderate-low**

- **Value:** The November 17 screen showed 10.6x trailing earnings and a 12.6% FCF yield. The price may understate normalized earnings if its branded checkout franchise stabilizes; that is a thesis, not a forecast.
- **Quality:** The two-sided payments platform had 440 million active accounts and $487.1 billion in quarterly payment volume. Third-quarter operating cash flow was $1.6 billion and FCF was $1.4 billion; cash, equivalents and investments were $15.1 billion against $13.5 billion of debt at September 30. [PayPal's quarterly filing exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000086/pypl3q-26earningsrelease.htm).
- **Disconfirmation:** Competitive pressure on branded checkout remains substantial. Revenue grew only 2% year over year, GAAP operating income fell 8%, and quarterly FCF fell 17%. BNPL receivable timing affects cash flow; adjusted FCF excludes a timing item and is not interchangeable with reported FCF. [Competition context](https://apnews.com/article/50054e5db0c773c8fe9a437708e1d3a9).

### Today's top ideas

**1. Adobe (NASDAQ: ADBE) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** The November 20 large-company screen showed 12.4x earnings. If its recurring franchise retains pricing power and customers, this multiple may undervalue sustained cash generation; this is an inference, not a valuation target.
- **Quality:** Fiscal Q3 revenue rose 13% year over year to $6.76 billion; GAAP operating income was $2.35 billion and operating cash flow was $2.52 billion. Annualized recurring revenue was $27.50 billion. Creative workflows, installed user base and subscription relationships support durability, though not an unassailable moat. [SEC earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000147/adbeex991q326.htm).
- **Risks and tests:** Generative AI competitors could weaken pricing or make workflows less sticky, while model costs and stock compensation can reduce owner economics. Watch renewal and recurring-revenue growth, GAAP margins and cash flow after dilution. Cash flow from operations is not FCF.

**2. Lennox International (NYSE: LII) | Confidence: moderate**

- **Value:** The S&P screen displays 15.4x earnings and a 7.2% FCF yield, subject to the stale-data caveat above. The price could be reasonable for a profitable HVAC franchise if earnings normalize rather than erode.
- **Quality:** Q3 revenue rose 10% to $1.57 billion; quarterly operating cash flow was $395 million and FCF was $363 million, helped by lower inventory. Its equipment and service relationships provide a replacement-market opportunity. [Company Q3 results](https://investor.lennox.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lennox-reports-2026-third-quarter-earnings-results).
- **Risks and tests:** Adjusted EPS fell 7% and segment profit fell 2% despite revenue growth. Housing demand, weather, tariffs, cost inflation and acquisition integration can compress margins. Inventory release is not a repeatable cash-flow engine; verify leverage and normalized cash generation.

**3. Zoetis (NYSE: ZTS) | Confidence: moderate-low**

- **Value:** The S&P screen displays 13.3x earnings and 6.8% FCF yield, subject to the stale-data caveat. A discount could be warranted if animal-health growth remains impaired; the case depends on stabilization, not a guaranteed recovery.
- **Quality:** A diversified animal-health portfolio spans medicines, vaccines and diagnostics. Q3 revenue was $2.4 billion and reported net income $591 million, demonstrating continuing profitability. [Company Q3 results](https://news.zoetis.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Zoetis-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-Highlights-Progress-Advancing-Innovation-Across-Diversified-Portfolio-and-Narrows-Full-Year-2026-Revenue-Guidance-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Adjusted-EPS-Guidance/).
- **Risks and tests:** Q3 revenue fell 1% and reported net income fell 18%. Competition, generics and product safety concerns could persist. Management's full-year organic operational revenue outlook calls for a 1%-2% decline. Watch companion-animal sales and underlying profit, not merely adjusted EPS boosted by repurchases.

**4. PayPal Holdings (NASDAQ: PYPL) | Confidence: moderate-low**

- **Value:** The S&P screen displays 10.6x earnings and a 12.6% FCF yield, subject to the stale-data caveat. The discount could prove attractive if branded checkout economics stabilize, but a structurally lower margin could justify it.
- **Quality:** PayPal reported 440 million active accounts, Q3 operating cash flow of $1.6 billion and FCF of $1.43 billion. Cash, equivalents and investments were $15.1 billion versus $13.5 billion debt at September 30; that simple comparison does not establish unrestricted excess cash. [SEC earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000086/pypl3q-26earningsrelease.htm).

Q3 FCF fell 17% year over year; adjusted FCF also fell 10% and excludes BNPL receivable timing. Monitor branded checkout, transaction margins and cash conversion through a full credit and funding cycle.
