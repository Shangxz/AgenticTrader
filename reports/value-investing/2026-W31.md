## Today's Top Ideas

### 1. Microsoft (MSFT)

**Why it looks undervalued:** Microsoft is not optically cheap in an absolute sense, but it appears unusually reasonable for a business still compounding revenue, operating income, and cloud backlog at high rates. Around this run, public quote data put MSFT near $389 with a P/E around the low-20s, while recent Morningstar-cited commentary described the shares as materially below fair value after a 2026 pullback. The setup is a quality compounder at a market-like multiple rather than a deep-value stock.

**Why it still qualifies as quality:** In fiscal Q3 2026, revenue rose 18% to $82.9 billion, operating income rose 20% to $38.4 billion, diluted EPS rose 23% to $4.27, Microsoft Cloud revenue rose 29% to $54.5 billion, and Azure plus other cloud services grew 40%. Commercial remaining performance obligation rose 99% to $627 billion, a strong signal of future contracted demand.

**Main risks or disconfirming factors:** Microsoft reports fiscal Q4 after this run, on July 29, 2026, so near-term earnings risk is high. AI spending, cloud capacity allocation, and possible disruption to productivity software and developer tools remain key disconfirming questions. The thesis weakens if AI growth requires structurally lower free cash flow conversion.

**Notable valuation signals:** Low-20s earnings multiple for a company with roughly 40% Azure growth and high operating margins. Strongest current candidate among megacap quality franchises because the valuation is not pricing a heroic margin expansion story.

Source: [Microsoft FY26 Q3 results](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q3/press-release-webcast), [Microsoft investor relations](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/default)

### 2. Zoetis (ZTS)

**Why it looks undervalued:** Zoetis has been repriced sharply after slower pet-care demand and more competition. The stock closed July 27 at $76.92, more than 50% below its 52-week high cited by MarketWatch. Against Zoetis' revised 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $6.85 to $7.00, the market price implies a low-teens multiple for a leading animal-health franchise.

**Why it still qualifies as quality:** Zoetis remains the global animal-health leader with a diversified companion-animal and livestock portfolio. Even in a difficult Q1, the company generated $2.3 billion of revenue, $601 million of net income, and $646 million of adjusted net income. Management also pointed to a pipeline of more than 12 potential blockbuster products.

**Main risks or disconfirming factors:** This is the most turnaround-sensitive idea on the list. Q1 organic operational revenue was flat, US companion-animal sales declined, and management cited price sensitivity, lower vet visits, and competition in dermatology and parasiticides. If these are structural rather than cyclical pressures, the low multiple is deserved.

**Notable valuation signals:** Low-teens multiple on management's adjusted EPS guidance, with wide-moat characteristics still plausible but less certain than in prior years. Confidence: medium, due to real competitive pressure.

Sources: [Zoetis Q1 2026 SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1555280/000155528026000021/exhibit99q12026.htm), [MarketWatch ZTS July 27 recap](https://www.marketwatch.com/data-news/zoetis-inc-stock-outperforms-competitors-on-strong-trading-day-f16fed6a-d8e40169d955)

### 3. Berkshire Hathaway (BRK.B)

**Why it looks undervalued:** Berkshire is not a dramatic discount, but it remains one of the cleaner quality-value anchors in a market priced above historical averages. Around this run, Morningstar quote data showed BRK.B near the high-$490s and about 1.47 times book value. That is a reasonable price for a diversified collection of insurance, railroad, energy, manufacturing, service, and marketable-securities assets with a fortress balance sheet.

**Why it still qualifies as quality:** Berkshire's first-quarter report showed broad operating resilience: after-tax insurance underwriting earnings increased to $1.7 billion, BNSF earnings rose 13.4%, BHE earnings rose 1.5%, and manufacturing/service/retail earnings rose 4.5%. Insurance investment income remained substantial even though lower interest rates reduced it year over year.

**Main risks or disconfirming factors:** Berkshire's cash-heavy profile can lag in strong bull markets. Succession and capital allocation after Warren Buffett remain important investor concerns, and insurance results can be volatile. Price-to-book is useful but imperfect because the operating businesses differ meaningfully from the marketable securities portfolio.

**Notable valuation signals:** Roughly 1.5 times book for a high-quality, low-leverage conglomerate. Confidence: medium-high as a conservative value anchor, lower as a high-upside idea.

Source: [Berkshire Hathaway Q1 2026 report](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/qtrly/1stqtr26.pdf), [Morningstar BRK.B quote](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/xnys/brk.b/quote)

### 4. Qualcomm (QCOM)

**Why it looks undervalued:** Qualcomm screens as a more classic value candidate: the stock was recently near $170, with public quote data showing a high-teens P/E and a share price about one-third below its 52-week high. That valuation is modest if handset weakness stabilizes and automotive/IoT diversification continues.

**Why it still qualifies as quality:** Qualcomm's fiscal Q2 2026 revenue was $10.6 billion, non-GAAP EPS was $2.65, and combined QCT automotive and IoT revenue grew 20% year over year. The company completed $5.4 billion of repurchases in the first half of fiscal 2026 and announced a new $20 billion authorization.

**Main risks or disconfirming factors:** Revenue declined year over year in fiscal Q2, non-GAAP EPS fell, handset cyclicality remains important, and the stock is exposed to semiconductor geopolitics, China demand, and customer concentration. This is cheaper than most quality tech, but with more cyclical risk.

**Notable valuation signals:** High-teens P/E, shareholder returns, and diversification growth. Confidence: medium.

Sources: [Qualcomm Q2 fiscal 2026 SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432826000060/qcom032926erex991.htm), [MarketWatch QCOM July 27 recap](https://www.marketwatch.com/data-news/qualcomm-inc-stock-outperforms-competitors-on-strong-trading-day-c548e739-a70294a898ac)

### 5. Comcast (CMCSA)

**Why it looks undervalued:** Comcast is cheap because investors are heavily discounting broadband erosion. Q2 free cash flow was $4.6 billion, and the company returned $2.1 billion to shareholders. The market is assigning a low value to those cash flows because the core broadband subscriber base is shrinking and the company is preparing to separate NBCUniversal and Sky.

**Why it still qualifies as quality:** Despite clear pressure, Comcast still generates large free cash flow, owns valuable connectivity and media assets, and is growing wireless lines. Q2 domestic wireless net additions reached 448,000, while broadband losses improved year over year.

**Main risks or disconfirming factors:** This is the closest name on the list to a value-trap risk. Domestic residential broadband losses remain material, broadband revenue declined, and management paused share repurchases while working through the separation. If fixed wireless, fiber, and satellite competition permanently reset broadband economics, the low valuation is not enough.

**Notable valuation signals:** Large current free cash flow against a deeply pressured equity price, but with structural risk higher than the other top ideas. Confidence: medium-low.

Source: [Comcast Q2 2026 results](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260723764612/en/Comcast-Reports-2nd-Quarter-2026-Results)

## Short Conclusion

The strongest current quality-value candidates are Microsoft, Zoetis, Berkshire Hathaway, Qualcomm, and Comcast, in that order. Microsoft and Berkshire look like the cleaner quality anchors; Zoetis and Qualcomm offer more upside if recent pressure proves temporary; Comcast is statistically cheap but carries the highest structural-risk burden. Alphabet remains high quality, but today's fresh screen moved it to watchlist because AI capex and financing risk make the valuation signal less clean.
