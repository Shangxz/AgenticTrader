### 1. Adobe (ADBE)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $264.02, Adobe trades at about **14.7x** the midpoint of FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance of $17.90-$18.00 and **10.8x** midpoint non-GAAP guidance of $24.35-$24.45. The GAAP multiple is the more conservative anchor because stock compensation is a recurring economic cost. Even on that basis, the valuation is modest for a subscription software leader whose customer-group subscription revenue grew 12% in constant currency in fiscal Q2.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Adobe's creative formats, workflows, installed base, professional training ecosystem and Document Cloud distribution create meaningful switching costs. Q2 total customer-group subscription revenue rose 14% reported, Business Professionals & Consumers rose 16%, and Creative & Marketing Professionals rose 13%. Full-year guidance calls for 10.2% ending ARR growth. High gross margins, recurring billing and a falling share count support per-share cash economics. [Adobe Q2 FY2026 release and guidance](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/11606202/a5543arefgt.pdf)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Generative AI can broaden Adobe's market, but it can also lower creation costs, strengthen point competitors and weaken traditional workflow lock-in. Stock compensation was about 8% of first-half revenue, the Semrush acquisition contributed to reported growth, and Adobe is operating with an interim CFO. The thesis weakens if organic ARR growth drops below high single digits, AI monetization fails to offset competitive pressure, or buybacks merely absorb dilution rather than create per-share value.

### 2. Accenture (ACN)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $176.89, Accenture trades at about **13.2x** midpoint FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance of $13.38-$13.50. Management's $10.8-$11.5 billion free-cash-flow outlook equates to roughly a **10.2% FCF yield** on the current equity value. The small GAAP/adjusted EPS gap makes the valuation cleaner than many software comparisons.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Accenture has deep relationships with large enterprises, broad technical and industry expertise, global delivery scale and a capital-light model. Fiscal Q3 revenue grew 3% in local currency, operating margin expanded 20 basis points to 17.0%, EPS rose 9%, and FCF was $3.6 billion. The company had 104 quarterly bookings of at least $100 million year to date, up 13%, evidence that it remains central to complex transformation programs. [Accenture Q3 FY2026 release](https://investor.accenture.com/~/media/Files/A/accenture-v4/investors/earnings-reports/2026/accenture-3q-fy26-earnings-release.pdf)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** New bookings fell 3% in local currency, consulting growth was only 1%, and management narrowed full-year local-currency revenue growth to 3%-4%. AI may expand transformation demand while simultaneously compressing labor-based fees and delivery headcount. A raised acquisition budget adds integration and capital-allocation risk. Persistent bookings contraction, no improvement beyond low-single-digit organic growth, or FCF consumed by acquisitions without per-share returns would challenge the case.

### 3. Deckers Outdoor (DECK)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $93.09, Deckers trades near **12.5x** midpoint FY2027 diluted EPS guidance of $7.35-$7.50. At June 30 it held **$1.60 billion of cash and no outstanding borrowings**, unusually strong downside support for a consumer brand company. The current price is also below the $103.79 average paid for first-quarter repurchases, although management's purchase price is evidence, not proof, of value.

**Why it remains a quality business.** HOKA and UGG have premium positioning, strong direct-to-consumer reach and growing international demand. Fiscal Q1 revenue rose 5.7%, DTC revenue rose 13%, international revenue rose 8.4%, and gross margin improved 60 basis points to 56.4%. Inventories fell year over year, reducing one common source of apparel and footwear risk. [Deckers Q1 FY2027 release](https://ir.deckers.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000910521-26-000015/deckex991pressrelease-6302.htm)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Brand heat is not permanent. SG&A grew faster than revenue, operating income declined 6%, domestic growth was only 3.2%, and HOKA plus UGG concentration makes product execution crucial. Tariffs, fashion shifts, wholesale discounting and aggressive repurchases can destroy value if demand weakens. The thesis weakens with sustained negative comparable sales, gross-margin erosion or inventory growth that materially outruns revenue.

### 4. Broadridge Financial Solutions (BR)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $171.01, Broadridge trades at **17.8x** FY2026 EPS of $9.60. Applying management's 8%-12% FY2027 EPS-growth range implies roughly **16.1-16.5x** next year's earnings. This is reasonable rather than deep value, but the cash-flow durability and growth visibility make the multiple more attractive than it first appears.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Broadridge is embedded in mandatory investor communications, governance and capital-markets workflows where regulation, scale and switching costs support recurring revenue. FY2026 recurring revenue grew 8%, adjusted EPS grew 12%, free-cash-flow conversion reached 110%, and closed sales rose to $305 million. FY2027 guidance calls for 6%-8% recurring growth, more than 100% FCF conversion and another 8%-12% of EPS growth. [Broadridge FY2026 results](https://s203.q4cdn.com/209299927/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/EX99-1-Earnings-Release-4Q-2026.pdf)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Acquisitions contributed to growth, Wealth and Investment Management grew only 1% in Q4, and lower-quality distribution revenue benefits from postage increases. Client concentration, cyber incidents, systems failures, financial-market activity and digital-asset investments all matter. The case weakens if organic recurring growth falls below mid-single digits, conversion drops below 100%, or acquisition-led growth fails to lift per-share cash flow.

### 5. Zoetis (ZTS)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $73.79, Zoetis trades at about **10.7x** midpoint 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $6.85-$7.00 and about **12.1x trailing GAAP EPS**. That is a distressed valuation for the leading listed animal-health company and offers substantial re-rating potential if current weakness proves temporary.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Zoetis has trusted brands, regulated products, veterinarian relationships, broad global distribution and a portfolio spanning companion animals and livestock. These advantages have historically supported repeat demand and attractive margins. Even reduced guidance still anticipates 2%-5% organic revenue growth and 2%-6% organic adjusted-net-income growth. [Zoetis quarterly results and current guidance](https://investor.zoetis.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** The discount is not free: guidance has been reduced, US companion-animal demand has softened, competition and market-share questions have increased, and debt is materially higher than at Deckers or Accenture. Product safety, veterinary traffic, channel inventories and livestock cycles also matter. Confidence is only medium until organic growth stabilizes. Continued share loss, another guidance reduction, near-zero organic growth or rising leverage would invalidate the recovery thesis.

At about 21.8x guided GAAP EPS, however, the discount is smaller than today's five; a 17% workforce reduction also raises execution questions. [Intuit Q3 FY2026 results](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance)

[Cigna Q2 2026 results](https://s202.q4cdn.com/757723766/files/doc_earnings/2026/q2/earnings-result/the-cigna-group-second-quarter-2026-release.pdf)
- **NXP Semiconductors (NXPI):** differentiated auto/industrial chips and strong recent FCF, but a cyclical recovery makes normalized earnings more uncertain.
- **Salesforce (CRM):** recurring software economics at a reset non-GAAP multiple, tempered by stock compensation, acquisition effects and debt-funded repurchases.
- **Arch Capital (ACGL):** excellent underwriting culture and book-value compounding, but current earnings benefit from the insurance cycle.
- **Ameriprise Financial (AMP):** high-return wealth economics and strong capital return, offset by sensitivity to asset markets and fund flows.
- **Comcast (CMCSA):** high current cash yield and separation optionality, with the clearest structural value-trap risk in broadband.
- **Nike (NKE):** globally valuable brand assets, but earnings erosion and turnaround execution remain too uncertain for the top group.

## Conclusion

**Adobe and Accenture are today's strongest quality-value combinations.** Adobe pairs the better growth profile with a conservative 14.7x guided GAAP multiple; Accenture offers the stronger cash-yield and balance-sheet case. **Deckers** supplies net-cash consumer exposure at a low-teens guided multiple, **Broadridge** is the cleanest recurring infrastructure compounder, and **Zoetis** has the largest potential re-rating but also the most immediate operating uncertainty.

No intrinsic-value estimate is certain. Treat this report as a research shortlist and test every thesis against its disconfirming factors rather than as a guarantee of investment returns.
