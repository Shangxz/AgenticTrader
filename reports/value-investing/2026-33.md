### 1. Accenture (ACN)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $178.25, Accenture trades for roughly **13.2x** the midpoint of FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance of $13.38-$13.50. Management guides to **$10.8-$11.5 billion of free cash flow**, equal to approximately a **9.8%-10.5% yield** on the current $109.7 billion market capitalization. The company also raised planned FY2026 repurchases to $7.5 billion.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Fiscal Q3 revenue rose 6% in US dollars to $18.7 billion, operating margin expanded 20 basis points to 17.0%, EPS rose 9%, and the quarter produced $3.6 billion of free cash flow. Accenture serves about 9,000 clients and remains deeply embedded in large enterprise transformation programs, with 104 bookings of at least $100 million year to date. [Accenture Q3 FY2026 results](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-reports-third-quarter-fiscal-2026-results) | [Repurchase increase](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/accenture-significantly-increases-fiscal-year-2026-share-repurchase-program-by-2-billion)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Local-currency growth is only 3%-4%, new bookings declined modestly year over year, and US federal work is a headwind. Generative AI may reduce demand for labor-intensive implementation even as it creates new transformation work. The thesis weakens if bookings and organic growth continue to decelerate, FCF misses guidance, or acquisitions become the main source of growth.

### 2. Adobe (ADBE)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $272.96, Adobe trades at roughly **15.2x** the midpoint of FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance of $17.90-$18.00, or about 15.6x trailing earnings. Q2 produced $2.17 billion of operating cash flow against only $58 million of capital spending, although one quarter should not be annualized mechanically.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Q2 revenue rose **13%** to a record $6.62 billion, subscription revenue grew 14%, ARR reached $27.10 billion, and GAAP operating income was $2.24 billion. Adobe raised full-year revenue and EPS targets, while AI-first ARR more than tripled and exceeded $500 million. The creative and document ecosystems retain distribution, workflow and switching-cost advantages that are difficult to replicate quickly. [Adobe Q2 FY2026 release](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/11606202/a5543arefgt.pdf)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Generative AI could reduce switching costs, increase inference costs or pressure pricing. Stock-based compensation was $534 million in Q2, so non-GAAP EPS materially overstates the cash economics available to owners. Semrush integration and the CFO transition add execution risk. ARR growth falling into single digits would materially weaken the case.

### 3. Deckers Outdoor (DECK)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $97.44, Deckers trades at roughly **13.1x** the midpoint of updated FY2027 EPS guidance of $7.35-$7.50. FY2026 free cash flow exceeded $1 billion. With $1.60 billion of cash and no outstanding borrowings at June 30, current enterprise value is about $11.9 billion, or less than 12x the prior year's FCF.

**Why it remains a quality business.** FY2026 revenue grew 9.8%, operating income reached $1.26 billion, EPS grew 11%, and HOKA and UGG sales grew 15.9% and 8.2%, respectively. In fiscal Q1 2027, revenue still grew 5.7%, inventories declined year over year, and management raised EPS guidance. The balance sheet and full-price brand strategy give Deckers room to invest and repurchase shares through a softer cycle. [Deckers FY2026 results](https://ir.deckers.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/656/deckers-brands-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-fiscal-year-2026-financial-results) | [Deckers Q1 FY2027 results](https://ir.deckers.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000910521-26-000015/deckex991pressrelease-6302.htm)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Fashion and footwear preferences can shift abruptly, HOKA and UGG account for nearly all revenue, and tariffs or freight costs can compress margins. Q1 operating income declined despite revenue growth. The thesis weakens if HOKA growth drops below management's low-double-digit outlook, full-price sell-through deteriorates, or SG&A growth persistently outruns sales.

### 4. Arch Capital Group (ACGL)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $98.25, Arch trades near **7.7x trailing earnings** and approximately **1.44x** June 30 book value of $68.04 per share. The low earnings multiple should not be treated as permanent because insurance profits are cyclical, but the book-value premium is reasonable for a company earning a mid-teens operating ROE.

**Why it remains a quality business.** Q2 produced a 15.3% annualized operating return on common equity, an 82.5% combined ratio excluding catastrophes and prior-year development, and a 2.8% sequential increase in book value per share. Arch repurchased $1.2 billion of stock in the quarter, and its specialty insurance, reinsurance and mortgage platforms diversify the underwriting engine. [Arch Capital Q2 2026 results](https://ir.archgroup.com/news/news-details/2026/Arch-Capital-Group-Ltd--Reports-2026-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)

**Risks / disconfirming factors.** Net premiums earned declined 8.1%, underwriting income fell 19.7%, and the adjusted combined ratio worsened 1.6 points year over year. Catastrophes, reserve errors and softening pricing can make trailing earnings an unreliable anchor. Sustained combined ratios above the low 90s or falling book value would challenge the thesis.

## Changes from August 10

- **Adobe remains in the leading group**, moving from rank 1 to rank 2 only because Accenture's current guided FCF yield provides a slightly stronger margin of safety. Adobe's operating evidence remains intact.
- **Accenture, Deckers and Arch Capital are new additions** after today's independent screen. Each offers a clearer combination of current earnings or cash yield, balance-sheet support and durable competitive position than yesterday's lower-ranked names.
- **Cigna, Qualcomm, Zoetis and Comcast leave today's top list.** This is a relative reranking, not an automatic sell conclusion. Cigna's August 13 update is still pending; Qualcomm is less cheap; Zoetis needs evidence of demand stabilization; and Comcast's leverage and legacy-network decline reduce its quality score.
- **Alphabet remains excluded despite an approximately 18x reported P/E.** Its Q2 operating momentum was excellent, but $44.9 billion of quarterly capex drove free cash flow negative, making the current earnings multiple a poor stand-alone measure of owner economics.

## Watchlist and near misses

- **Cigna (CI):** attractive near 9x prior adjusted EPS guidance, but the imminent Q2 report can materially change the evidence set.
- **Comcast (CMCSA):** 8.1x trailing earnings and strong cash generation, offset by about $90 billion of debt and structural residential-connectivity pressure.
- **PayPal (PYPL):** about 11.2x trailing earnings, but branded-checkout competition and weaker pricing power keep confidence below the leaders.
- **Alphabet (GOOGL):** exceptional Search and Cloud economics, but current AI infrastructure spending suppresses the FCF yield and widens valuation uncertainty.
- **The Trade Desk (TTD):** the collapse to roughly 16x trailing earnings creates upside if execution stabilizes, but an 85% drawdown and platform-transition questions demand more evidence before treating historical quality as durable.

## Conclusion

**Accenture is today's strongest balance of price, cash yield and durable enterprise relevance.** **Adobe** offers faster current growth at a still-compressed multiple, but AI competition and stock compensation deserve close monitoring. **Deckers** pairs brand strength with a debt-free balance sheet and shareholder yield, while **Arch Capital** is the cheapest statistically but requires the greatest normalization of cyclical earnings.

No intrinsic-value estimate is certain. Treat this report as a research shortlist and test each thesis against its disconfirming factors rather than as a guarantee of investment returns.
