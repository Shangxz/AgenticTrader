### 1. Adobe (ADBE)

## Current conclusion

**Best overall quality-value balance:** Adobe. A near-12% trailing cash yield, double-digit subscription growth, high margins, and active share retirement provide the clearest present-value case, with AI disruption the central uncertainty.

**Best cash-yield alternatives:** Accenture and Leidos. Accenture has the cleaner balance sheet and broader commercial moat; Leidos has the lower earnings multiple and stronger backlog visibility. Accenture's October 1 report is the immediate checkpoint.

**Best lower-multiple healthcare transition:** Bristol Myers Squibb. Current cash generation is compelling, but only if the Growth Portfolio and pipeline offset legacy erosion.

Cirrus Logic and Exelixis are the smaller, higher-concentration ideas. Both have net cash and strong margins, but their customer or product dependence warrants lower confidence and tighter thesis monitoring.

### 6. Nike (NKE)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $35.75, Nike trades at about 17 times FY26 GAAP EPS of $2.10 and holds $9.0 billion in cash and short-term investments. Morningstar ranked Nike the most undervalued constituent in its wide-moat focus list, at a stated 62% discount to fair value on September 21.

**Why it is still a quality business.** Nike remains a globally scaled athletic brand with unmatched sports-marketing reach and broad wholesale distribution. FY26 revenue held flat on a reported basis, wholesale revenue grew 6%, gross margin edged up to 42.9%, and North America partly offset weakness elsewhere. ## Conclusion

**Best current balance of quality and value:** Adobe. Its roughly 13x GAAP multiple, near-10% cash yield, double-digit subscription growth, high margins, and aggressive share retirement provide the clearest combination of present economics and durable franchise quality.

**Best steadier compounder:** Broadridge. Its valuation discount is smaller, but recurring revenue, cash conversion, and forward growth guidance make the underwriting cleaner.

**Best industrial-software and installed-base alternatives:** PTC and Otis. Both offer defensible recurring revenue at mid-single-digit cash yields, with company-specific issues that are visible and monitorable.

Constellation and Nike offer larger perceived discounts but require more confidence in consumer-demand stabilization. Position sizing, a long horizon, and confirmation from future results matter more for those two.
