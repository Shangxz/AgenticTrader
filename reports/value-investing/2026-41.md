### 1. Adobe (ADBE)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $242.27, Adobe trades at about 13.3x the midpoint of management's FY2026 GAAP EPS target of $18.12-$18.17. That is a substantial discount to the broad market for a business that still produced 13% revenue growth, record quarterly operating cash flow of $2.52 billion, and meaningful share retirement.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Q3 ARR reached $27.50 billion, subscription revenue grew 14%, GAAP operating margin was about 35%, and AI-first ARR grew more than 150% from a smaller base. Adobe's more than one billion monthly active users and embedded creative, document, and marketing workflows provide distribution and switching-cost advantages. Source: [Adobe Q3 FY2026 results](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/01906202/au56y4ter.pdf).

**Main risks / disconfirming factors.** Generative AI could compress pricing, reduce professional-seat demand, or shift value toward new platforms. The thesis weakens if total ARR growth falls to low single digits, net retention deteriorates, or repurchases stop reducing the share count.

### 2. Deckers Outdoor (DECK)

**Why it looks undervalued.** Deckers trades at about 11.2x the midpoint of FY2027 EPS guidance of $7.35-$7.50. Its $1.60 billion of cash equals roughly 14% of the current market value, and the company reported no outstanding borrowings. The price therefore offers both a high earnings yield and balance-sheet protection.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Q1 sales rose 5.7%, with HOKA up 7.7%, UGG up 4.9%, direct-to-consumer sales up 13%, and gross margin improving to 56.4%. Inventory fell year over year, international sales grew 8.4%, and management raised EPS guidance. These are not the operating signals of a brand franchise already in collapse. Source: [Deckers Q1 FY2027 results](https://ir.deckers.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/661/deckers-brands-reports-first-quarter-fiscal-year-2027-financial-results).

**Main risks / disconfirming factors.** Footwear demand is fashion-sensitive, HOKA growth has slowed, UGG is seasonal, and tariffs or wholesale inventory corrections can pressure margins. Q1 operating income fell despite higher sales because SG&A grew quickly. The thesis weakens if full-price demand fades, HOKA loses share, or buybacks merely offset declining operating profit.

### 3. The Cigna Group (CI)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $282.43, Cigna trades at about 9.3x management's 2026 adjusted EPS outlook of at least $30.45 and about 11.7x trailing GAAP earnings. The valuation provides a large earnings-yield advantage to the market without requiring rapid growth.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Q2 revenue rose 7% to $71.7 billion and adjusted income increased about 6% to $2.05 billion. Specialty and Care Services adjusted operating income grew 22%, while Cigna Healthcare adjusted operating income rose 17%. Scale across pharmacy benefits, specialty care, and employer health creates purchasing, data, and distribution advantages. Source: [Cigna Q2 2026 results and outlook](https://newsroom.thecignagroup.com/2026-07-30-The-Cigna-Group-Reports-Strong-Second-Quarter-2026-Results,-Raises-2026-Outlook).

**Main risks / disconfirming factors.** Pharmacy Benefit Services adjusted operating income fell 27% in Q2 as contract renewals and customer initiatives reset economics. PBM regulation, medical-cost inflation, reserve development, client concentration, debt, and heavy reliance on adjusted earnings are material risks. Persistent Evernorth margin compression without offsetting specialty and employer-health growth would undermine the case.

### 4. Accenture (ACN)

**Why it looks undervalued.** Accenture trades at about 14.3x the midpoint of FY2027 GAAP EPS guidance of $14.39-$14.81. FY2026 free cash flow of $11.62 billion implies a market-cap-to-FCF multiple near 11.1x. Those multiples are modest for a global, asset-light services franchise still growing revenue and earnings.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** FY2026 revenue grew 6% to $74.2 billion, GAAP EPS rose 12%, free cash flow increased to $11.6 billion, and bookings reached $84.5 billion with a 1.1x book-to-bill. Managed-services bookings carried a 1.2x book-to-bill, supporting recurring demand, while $12.8 billion of cash provides flexibility. Source: [Accenture FY2026 results and FY2027 outlook](https://newsroom.accenture.com/content/4q-full-fy26-earnings/accenture-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026-results.pdf).

**Main risks / disconfirming factors.** FY2027 local-currency revenue growth guidance is only 3%-6%. AI may reduce labor intensity faster than Accenture creates new higher-value work, and acquisitions or weak federal demand can dilute returns. The thesis needs bookings to convert into revenue, stable utilization and pricing, and evidence that revenue per employee rises through the AI transition.

Persistent outflows in retirement and multi-asset products would be a more serious warning than continued active-equity weakness alone.
