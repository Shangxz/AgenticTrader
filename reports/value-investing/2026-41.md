## Market context

On October 5, the 10-year Treasury yield was about 5.27% and the 30-year yield about 5.63%, both near multi-decade highs; meanwhile, the S&P 500 finished the prior session at 7,722.72 and still traded at just under 19 times forward earnings. That combination favors companies with visible cash generation and a genuine discount, not merely distant growth. ## How today's screen was run

Today's work began from a fresh ranking and did not use any prior report selections. The broad-market pass used a factor screen spanning roughly 4,600 U.S.-listed stocks, with about 1,500 sufficiently liquid names eligible, then cross-checked other quality-value screens. Final ranking emphasized earnings and free-cash-flow yield, recurring revenue or backlog, operating economics, balance-sheet resilience, and identifiable reasons for the discount. Financials and risks were then checked against company releases. Free-cash-flow yields use market capitalization and company-reported cash flow; they are approximations and should not be treated as normalized intrinsic-value estimates.

### 1. Intuit (INTU)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $281.08, the stock is about 13.9 times the midpoint of management's FY2027 GAAP EPS guidance of $20.12–$20.36. FY2026 operating cash flow was $8.84 billion and property/equipment purchases were $0.22 billion, implying roughly $8.62 billion of simple free cash flow, or about 11.1% of the current $77.6 billion market value.

**Why it is still a quality business.** FY2026 revenue rose 14% to $21.4 billion, GAAP operating income rose 20%, and GAAP EPS rose 20%. Online Ecosystem revenue rose 19%; QuickBooks Online Accounting grew 23%. Tax and accounting workflows are recurring, data-rich, and costly for customers to replace. Management also repurchased $5.5 billion of stock, more than offsetting stock-based-compensation dilution. [Intuit FY2026 results and FY2027 guidance](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1320/intuit-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026-results-sets-fiscal-2027-guidance).

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** FY2027 revenue growth guidance slows to 9%–10%; TurboTax unit volumes fell 2% in FY2026 and Mailchimp guidance is flat to down slightly. FY2026 cash flow benefited from large non-cash deferred-tax and stock-compensation add-backs, so the headline FCF yield is not a clean owner-earnings yield. Credit Karma adds macro and credit-cycle sensitivity.

### 2. Adobe (ADBE)

**Why it looks undervalued.** The $237.69 price is about 13.1 times Adobe's raised FY2026 GAAP EPS target of $18.12–$18.17, a low multiple for a subscription software franchise still growing revenue at a double-digit rate.

**Why it is still a quality business.** Q3 revenue increased 13% to $6.76 billion, subscription revenue grew 14%, GAAP operating margin was about 35%, and operating cash flow reached $2.52 billion. ARR was $27.5 billion, the company passed one billion monthly active users, and AI-first ARR grew more than 150%. These figures suggest Adobe's distribution and workflow position remain substantial despite generative-AI disruption. [Adobe Q3 FY2026 release](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/01906202/au56y4ter.pdf).

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** Generative AI lowers creation costs and could weaken pricing power or shift value toward new platforms. Stock-based compensation was $544 million in Q3, and the CEO transition scheduled for December adds execution risk. The thesis breaks if ARR growth decelerates materially while AI costs rise.

### 3. Accenture (ACN)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $198.90, Accenture trades at about 13.4–13.8 times management's FY2027 GAAP EPS range of $14.39–$14.81. FY2026 free cash flow was $11.62 billion against a market value near $122.4 billion, an approximate 9.5% yield.

**Why it is still a quality business.** FY2026 revenue grew 6% to $74.2 billion, adjusted EPS grew 8%, annual bookings reached $84.5 billion, and Q4 book-to-bill was 1.2. The mix spans consulting and managed services, industries, and regions; that breadth plus embedded client relationships supports repeat business. Management returned $11.5 billion to shareholders during the year. [Accenture FY2026 results](https://newsroom.accenture.com/content/4q-full-fy26-earnings/accenture-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026-results.pdf).

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** FY2027 revenue guidance is only 3%–6% in local currency and FCF guidance of $11.0–$11.8 billion is roughly flat. AI may both create projects and compress labor-based pricing. Heavy buybacks are only attractive if the earnings base proves durable.

### 4. Leidos (LDOS)

**Why it looks undervalued.** The $117.74 price is roughly 11 times trailing earnings, well below the broad market despite positive revenue, backlog, and cash-flow trends.

**Why it is still a quality business.** Q2 revenue rose 7% to $4.6 billion, operating cash flow was $793 million, and non-GAAP free cash flow was $761 million. Quarterly awards were $4.9 billion, book-to-bill was 1.1, and backlog was $48.7 billion, including $10.2 billion funded. Security clearances, program knowledge, and long procurement cycles create meaningful switching costs. [Leidos Q2 2026 results](https://investors.leidos.com/news-releases/news-release-details/leidos-delivers-strong-second-quarter-and-enhances-full-year).

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** Debt was $6.0 billion versus $0.75 billion of cash at quarter end. Government budgets, procurement timing, fixed-price execution, and contract concentration can create abrupt earnings volatility. Q2 cash conversion was unusually strong and should not be annualized mechanically.

### 5. The Cigna Group (CI)

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $270.53, the shares trade at about 8.9 times management's 2026 adjusted EPS floor of $30.45 and about 11.2 times trailing GAAP earnings. The discount is large enough to compensate for some policy and medical-cost uncertainty, but not all of it.

**Why it is still a quality business.** Q2 revenue rose 7% to $71.7 billion and adjusted income from operations rose 6% to $2.05 billion. Evernorth and Cigna Healthcare provide diversification across pharmacy services and benefits, and the adjusted SG&A ratio improved to 4.6% from 4.9%. Management raised its full-year adjusted EPS outlook. [Cigna Q2 2026 release](https://app.boardroomalpha.com/sec_feed/2026/QTR3/0001140361-26-030135/ef20078875_ex99-1.htm).

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** Pharmacy-benefit-manager regulation, drug-pricing reform, utilization, medical-cost trend, reimbursement pressure, and political scrutiny can impair normalized margins. Adjusted earnings exclude amortization and special items; the GAAP valuation deserves more weight than the lower adjusted multiple.

### 6. Regeneron Pharmaceuticals (REGN)

**Why it looks undervalued.** The stock trades near 18.2 times trailing earnings, only around the broad market despite a much stronger balance sheet. Cash and marketable securities were $17.8 billion against only $2.0 billion of long-term debt, leaving net liquidity near 20% of the current $77.9 billion market value.

**Why it is still a quality business.** Q2 revenue increased 17% to $4.3 billion. Dupixent global sales grew 38%, EYLEA HD U.S. sales grew 52%, and Libtayo sales grew 30%. The Sanofi development balance was fully repaid, improving the economics of future collaboration profit, while the pipeline provides multiple additional shots on goal. [Regeneron Q2 2026 results](https://investor.regeneron.com/node/32226/pdf).

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** Drug revenue is exposed to patent, reimbursement, trial, and regulatory outcomes. EYLEA faces intense competition, and even a strong pipeline can fail clinically. At 18 times earnings, Regeneron offers less valuation protection than the first five names; its balance sheet and growth earn it a place, but at lower confidence.

## Near misses and value traps to watch

- **Comcast (CMCSA):** About 7 times trailing earnings and $4.6 billion of Q2 FCF are enticing, but residential revenue and EBITDA declined, consolidated adjusted EBITDA fell 13.4%, capex rose, and buybacks were paused during the planned NBCUniversal/Sky separation. The discount is real, but so is the deterioration. [Q2 results](https://cmcsa.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/comcast-reports-2nd-quarter-2026-results).
- **PayPal (PYPL):** Roughly 10 times trailing earnings with heavy repurchases, but Q2 non-GAAP operating income fell 8% and margin contracted 248 basis points. Cash generation alone does not yet prove the competitive moat has stabilized. [Q2 SEC earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000080/pypl2q-26earningsrelease.htm).
- **Bristol Myers Squibb (BMY):** The broad factor screen ranks it exceptionally well on value and quality, but looming patent losses make reported earnings a poor stand-alone guide to normalized value. Pipeline replacement must be underwritten drug by drug.
- **Exelixis (EXEL):** Strong margins and revenue growth, but the business remains heavily dependent on cabozantinib and lowered its 2026 revenue guidance range. That concentration keeps it outside the top six. [Q2 results](https://ir.exelixis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/exelixis-announces-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and).

## Conclusion

**Strongest current candidates: Intuit, Adobe, and Accenture.** Each combines a low-teens earnings multiple with durable recurring demand and strong cash generation. **Leidos** offers the best non-software combination of price and visibility. **Cigna** is the cheapest on forward earnings but carries the greatest policy and accounting-normalization risk. **Regeneron** has the best balance sheet and growth profile, but the narrowest valuation margin of safety.

The most useful disconfirming checks before committing capital are: whether Intuit's cash conversion normalizes lower, whether Adobe sustains ARR growth through the AI transition, whether Accenture bookings convert without margin pressure, and whether Cigna's medical-cost and policy environment stays within guidance.
