# Value Investing Weekly Report - 2026-W35

_Last updated: August 26, 2026 (prices observed around 8:00 a.m. ET). Research support only; not personal financial advice._

## Market context and method

The broad U.S. market still offers a limited aggregate margin of safety. The S&P 500 trades near 20 times forward earnings, while Morningstar's third-quarter work estimates the U.S. market at an 8% discount to its fair-value estimates and small-value stocks at a 21% discount. Mid- and small-cap aggregate multiples are lower than the large-cap index, but low multiples alone remain an inadequate defense against weak balance sheets or structural decline.

Today's assessment restarted from a broad screen of U.S.-listed large-, mid-, and small-cap operating companies. Candidate generation combined market-wide value lists and screens with earnings and free-cash-flow yield, returns and margins, leverage, competitive durability, and recent operating momentum. Financials and commodity producers were judged with sector-appropriate measures. This is a broad market screen, not a claim that every listed security received a full intrinsic-value model. Earlier weekly entries were used only to record changes after the current ranking was set.

Screening context: [Kiplinger undervalued-stock screen](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/best-undervalued-stocks), [Kiplinger quality-value list](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/the-best-value-stocks-to-buy), [Morningstar Q3 2026 valuation outlook](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/33-undervalued-stocks-buy-before-market-catches), and [Morningstar wide-moat value screen](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/10-undervalued-wide-moat-stocks-worth-buying-right-now).

### 1. Intuit (INTU) - High conviction

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $357.46 after the post-earnings decline, Intuit trades at roughly 17.7 times the midpoint of FY2027 GAAP EPS guidance of $20.12-$20.36. It trades near 15.5 times the midpoint of revised non-GAAP EPS guidance of $22.88-$23.12, which now includes stock-based compensation and is therefore more conservative than the company's prior adjusted definition. That is an unusual valuation for a subscription-heavy franchise still guiding to 9%-10% revenue growth and 22%-24% GAAP EPS growth.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Fiscal 2026 revenue rose 14% to $21.4 billion, GAAP operating income increased 20% to $5.9 billion, and GAAP EPS increased 20% to $16.46. Global Business Solutions grew 16%, QuickBooks Online Accounting grew 23%, and the company ended the year with $7.2 billion of cash and investments against $7.7 billion of debt. Tax and accounting workflow integration, proprietary financial data, high switching costs, and a large installed base support the durability case. [Intuit FY2026 results and FY2027 guidance](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1320/intuit-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026-results-sets-fiscal-2027-guidance)

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** FY2027 revenue growth is slowing from 14% to 9%-10%; Mailchimp is guided to a 1% decline to flat revenue; total U.S. TurboTax units fell 2%; and the recent 17% workforce reduction raises execution risk. Government tax-filing initiatives, AI-native accounting tools, or weaker small-business formation could impair growth. The thesis would weaken if QuickBooks Online growth decelerates sharply, margins fail to expand, or the restructuring does not produce durable operating leverage.

### 2. Adobe (ADBE) - High conviction

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $273.92, Adobe trades at roughly 15.3 times the midpoint of FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance of $17.90-$18.00 and 11.2 times the midpoint of non-GAAP guidance of $24.35-$24.45. The discount embeds a substantial probability that generative AI weakens Adobe's product moat or pricing power.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Fiscal Q2 revenue rose 13% to a record $6.62 billion, subscription revenue was $6.42 billion, GAAP operating income was $2.24 billion, and operating cash flow was $2.17 billion. Adobe raised full-year revenue and non-GAAP EPS targets, while AI-first annualized recurring revenue more than tripled and exceeded $500 million. Entrenched creative workflows, recurring revenue, high margins, and strong cash conversion remain meaningful defenses. [Adobe Q2 FY2026 results](https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/11606202/a5543arefgt.pdf)

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** AI-native creation tools may lower switching costs, compress pricing, or move value away from Adobe's applications. The Semrush acquisition contributes to reported growth, stock-based compensation remains material, and the CFO departed in June. Organic ARR falling below guidance, weak AI monetization, or structural margin decline would challenge the thesis.

### 3. The Cigna Group (CI) - High conviction

**Why it looks undervalued.** At $279.32, Cigna trades at about 9.2 times management's raised 2026 adjusted EPS outlook of at least $30.45 and about 11.6 times trailing GAAP earnings. The discount is large relative to the broad market even though management raised its outlook.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Second-quarter revenue increased 7% to $71.7 billion, adjusted income from operations rose to $2.1 billion, and adjusted EPS increased to $7.78 from $7.20. Cigna Healthcare pretax adjusted income grew 17%, while Specialty and Care Services profit grew 22%. Diversification across insurance, pharmacy benefits, specialty pharmacy, and care services helps offset weakness in any one line. [Cigna Q2 2026 results](https://newsroom.thecignagroup.com/2026-07-30-The-Cigna-Group-Reports-Strong-Second-Quarter-2026-Results%2C-Raises-2026-Outlook)

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** Pharmacy Benefit Services pretax adjusted income fell 27%, Evernorth's margin remains thin, and the medical care ratio rose to 84.5%. PBM regulation, drug-pricing reform, adverse medical-cost development, or leverage could justify the low multiple. A sustained decline in Evernorth profit or failure to control medical costs would impair the thesis.

### 4. EOG Resources (EOG) - Medium-high conviction

**Why it looks undervalued.** EOG generated $6.65 billion of free cash flow over the latest four reported quarters. Against a market capitalization near $77.7 billion, that is an indicative trailing free-cash-flow yield of roughly 8.6%. The shares also trade near 11.4 times trailing GAAP earnings. These are attractive signals, but commodity prices make trailing cash flow a poor stand-alone estimate of normalized value.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Second-quarter free cash flow was $2.80 billion, and EOG returned $1.8 billion through dividends and repurchases. Total production increased to 1.41 million barrels of oil equivalent per day, cash operating costs were better than guidance in key categories, and net debt fell to $3.0 billion, only 8.7% of total capitalization. A diversified, low-cost resource base and conservative balance sheet give EOG more resilience than the typical producer. [EOG Q2 2026 results](https://investors.eogresources.com/2026-08-04-EOG-Resources-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results)

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** The thesis is directly exposed to oil and gas prices, depletion, cost inflation, and capital discipline. Second-quarter cash generation benefited from higher oil prices, while debt remains above the prior year after the Encino acquisition. Value would be overstated if investors capitalize peak commodity cash flow or if acquired inventory underperforms.

### 5. NXP Semiconductors (NXPI) - High conviction on business, medium-high on valuation

**Why it looks undervalued.** Current consensus screens place NXP near 14.2 times forward earnings, below the broad market. Trailing 12-month non-GAAP free cash flow was $2.81 billion, about a 4.9% yield on the current market capitalization. This is a reasonable entry valuation for a high-margin embedded-semiconductor business, though not a deep-value price.

**Why it still qualifies as quality.** Q2 revenue grew 19% to $3.50 billion, GAAP gross margin was 57.3%, and GAAP operating margin reached 30.6%. Non-GAAP free cash flow was $791 million, or 22.6% of revenue. NXP's positions in automotive, industrial, connectivity, and secure-edge processing benefit from long product cycles and customer qualification requirements. The company also repaid $750 million of notes from available cash. [NXP Q2 2026 results](https://investors.nxp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nxp-semiconductors-reports-second-quarter-2026-results)

**Main risks / disconfirming evidence.** Automotive and industrial chip demand is cyclical; China exposure and export controls add geopolitical risk; and current margins may be above mid-cycle levels. Rising inventories, weaker design-win momentum, or a sustained drop in free-cash-flow conversion would reduce the apparent discount.

- **Salesforce (CRM):** The recurring-revenue franchise and cash economics remain attractive at about 14.6 times the midpoint of current FY2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance. However, Q2 FY2027 results are due after the August 26 close, so today's screen avoids relying on guidance that will soon be refreshed. This is an event-risk exclusion, not a conclusion that the business weakened. [Salesforce earnings date](https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Announces-Date-of-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Earnings-Release-and-Webcast/default.aspx)
- **Charles Schwab (SCHW):** Scale, low-cost distribution, asset gathering, and advice penetration offer durable growth, but the current roughly 15 times forward earnings valuation is less compelling than the selected names after accounting for rate and funding sensitivity.
- **Qualcomm (QCOM):** Licensing economics and edge-AI optionality are attractive, but handset concentration, Apple modem insourcing, China exposure, and a less dramatic valuation discount keep it below NXP today.
- **Zoetis (ZTS):** The animal-health franchise remains valuable, but 2026 organic revenue is expected to decline 1%-3%, adjusted net income 5%-9%, and U.S. companion-animal sales fell 11%. The estimate reset and competitive risks remain too high for the top five. [Zoetis Q2 2026 results](https://investor.zoetis.com/news/news-details/2026/Zoetis-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)
- **Travel + Leisure (TNL), United Airlines (UAL), and General Motors (GM):** Headline multiples are low, but financing, cyclicality, capital intensity, and balance-sheet sensitivity reduce earnings durability versus today's selections.
- **Banks and alternative asset managers:** Several low-multiple candidates emerged, but rate, credit, funding, and mark sensitivity make their earnings quality less comparable with the selected operating businesses.

## Conclusion

**Intuit and Adobe are today's strongest quality-value candidates.** Intuit's post-results valuation now offers the best combination of franchise durability, high operating returns, and guidance-based earnings support. Adobe remains unusually inexpensive for a double-digit recurring-revenue business, although AI disruption is a genuine disconfirming risk. Cigna has the clearest near-term earnings support at a single-digit adjusted multiple. EOG offers the strongest cash yield but requires conservative commodity normalization, while NXP provides high-quality industrial technology exposure at a reasonable rather than extreme discount.

This shortlist is a starting point for deeper diligence, not a prediction of near-term performance or a claim of certain intrinsic value.
